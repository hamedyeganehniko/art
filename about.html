The Journey

My Story

Born in London, Hana Yeganeh is a visual artist whose work explores the delicate tension between light and shadow, memory and dream. Her paintings have been exhibited internationally and are held in private collections across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

Trained at the Royal Academy of Arts, Hana's practice is rooted in the tradition of classical painting while embracing contemporary themes of identity, emotion, and the human experience.
